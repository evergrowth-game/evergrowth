# Future Plans & Ideas

## 1. Visual Boundary Markers
The 200-block town radius is currently invisible. Adding an item (e.g., a "Surveyor's Tool") that temporarily highlights the town's borders with particle effects would be a huge quality-of-life improvement for players trying to figure out where they can place deeds.

## 2. Inter-Town Trade Hubs
If players build multiple towns, introduce a "Trade Post" node that automatically ships excess items from one town's Depot to another town's Granary/Depot. This would allow players to build specialized settlements (e.g., a massive farming town feeding a distant mining town) and create functional logistics networks.
