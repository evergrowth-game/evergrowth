# Raider sounds

The upstream raider sound files are intentionally not included in Evergrowth.
Their individual licenses and source URLs could not be verified, so the raider
mobs currently have no mob-specific sound effects.

Future replacement sounds must include:

- the exact filename;
- the creator;
- the license, confirmed to permit redistribution in a free game;
- a direct source URL for verification.

The omitted upstream filenames were:

- `raiders_plunderercrossbow.ogg`
- `raiders_plunderercrossbow2.ogg`
- `raiders_plunderercrossbow3.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererflask.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererflask2.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererflask3.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererpirate.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererpirate2.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererpirate3.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererstick.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererstick2.ogg`
- `raiders_plundererstick3.ogg`
