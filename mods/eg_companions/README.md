# Evergrowth Companions (`eg_companions`)

`eg_companions` provides domestic humanoid companion NPCs for the Evergrowth game.

## Features

- **Domestic Companions:** Male and female domestic companions assigned to a player's home.
- **Companion Plaque:** A wallmounted gold plaque (`eg_companions:companion_plaque`) serving as the companion's daytime home base.
- **Dual-Tethering & Sleep Posture:**
  - **Daytime (05:30–18:30):** Wanders peacefully around the Companion Plaque.
  - **Nighttime (18:30–05:30):** Commutes to an assigned player-owned bed, executes mattress elevation offset (`-0.15`), and assumes a 90° pitch sleeping posture until morning.
- **Customization & Wardrobe Wand:** Punch a companion with the Wardrobe Wand to cycle through distinct male/female outfits.
- **Relocation Contracts:** Sneak + Right-Click a companion to safely package them into a Relocation Contract with their name, skin, health, and gender preserved.
